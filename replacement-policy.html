Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: March 2026

All Sales Are Final

By purchasing leads from JobsFuel, you acknowledge and agree that all payments are final and non-refundable. Because leads are generated and delivered in real time, fulfillment begins immediately upon payment. Low conversion rates, lack of closed deals, dissatisfaction with performance, or failure to generate revenue do not qualify for refunds or credits.

Lead Credits

While we do not offer refunds, we do offer lead credits in the following limited circumstances:

  • The phone number is disconnected or invalid
  • You reach a wrong person who has no connection to the request
  • The homeowner does not remember requesting an estimate or speaking with our representative

Lead credit requests must be submitted via text to your dedicated support number, including the lead name and reason for the request. All credit requests are reviewed and processed at the beginning of each week. JobsFuel reserves the right to make the final determination on whether a lead qualifies for a credit. This determination is made at JobsFuel's sole discretion and is not subject to appeal.

We Will NOT Credit a Lead If:

  • The homeowner wants to schedule work for a later date — this is still a valid opportunity
  • You have not completed the full follow-up procedure (minimum 5 call attempts and a text over 72 hours)
  • The homeowner receives multiple quotes and chooses another contractor
  • The homeowner changes their mind after speaking with you

We do not replace replacement leads or previously issued lead credits under any circumstances.

What Does Not Qualify for Refunds:

  • Low close rates or conversion rates
  • Leads that did not result in a booked or completed job
  • Dissatisfaction with lead quality outside of the criteria listed above
  • Change of mind after purchase

Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

By submitting payment, you authorize a legitimate charge for lead generation services rendered. You agree not to initiate any chargeback, payment dispute, or reversal with your bank or credit card provider without first sending written notice to info@tryjobsfuel.com and allowing at least seven (7) business days for resolution.

Initiating a chargeback without prior written notice constitutes a material breach of this agreement. In the event of a chargeback or dispute:

  • All lead delivery will be immediately suspended
  • You agree to reimburse any chargeback fees, administrative costs, and recovery expenses incurred by JobsFuel
  • JobsFuel reserves the right to pursue legal action to recover disputed amounts, including attorney's fees, court costs, and collection fees

Cancellations

JobsFuel operates on a pay-per-lead basis with no subscription. If you wish to pause or stop receiving leads, contact us via your dedicated support number and lead delivery will be paused within 24 hours. Note that frequently pausing your campaign may decrease lead quality and overall results. Consistent campaigns produce the best outcomes.

Disputes

For any disputes related to leads or payments, contact info@tryjobsfuel.com. We will make every reasonable effort to resolve issues fairly and promptly.
