The Process

How It Works

Three steps. Nothing hidden.

01

We reach out directly

We contact homeowners from our list one-on-one via text and call. Not ads. Not funnels. Personal outreach.

02

We confirm the estimate

We confirm every lead has a specific project in mind and genuinely wants an estimate. No tire-kickers. No "just browsing."

03

We hand you the lead exclusively

You get their name, number, address, and project details. You're the only contractor who gets it — we never share leads.

About

Our Mission

My name is Angus, and I started JobsFuel because I was tired of seeing contractors get ripped off by lead gen companies that charge retainers, share leads with four other guys, and disappear when things go wrong.

We've been at this for about three years. We're intentionally small — because when you scale too fast, you stop caring about individual clients. You start treating them like account numbers.

You deal with me directly. Not a ticket. Not a random rep. Me.

We're based out of Missoula, Montana. We work with contractors all across the US. If you want to know who you're dealing with before you do anything — call the number at the top of this page. I pick up.
